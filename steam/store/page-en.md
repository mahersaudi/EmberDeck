## About this game

[h2]Build the deck, then live with it[/h2]
Every run starts on the builder: thirty cards, chosen from everything you have unlocked, with a
copy limit that falls as a card gets rarer. There is no draft to hide behind — the deck you climb
with is the one you argued yourself into.

[h2]Heat[/h2]
Fire cards build Heat, and Heat stays between turns. Past the threshold it burns you at the end of
every turn, ignoring Block. Some cards spend it for an enormous effect. The whole game is the
question of how long you can hold a fire before it holds you.

[h2]Three acts, three bosses[/h2]
The Forge, the Obsidian Deep, and the Emberheart under both. Sixty cards, about thirty enemies,
relics, potions, shops, rest sites and events. Every enemy announces exactly what it will do next,
and the number it shows is the number that lands — after every modifier, with nothing hidden.

[h2]Losing is the game[/h2]
Every finished run — won or lost — earns Embers, which unlock new cards and relics for the decks
you build next. Five difficulty levels open one at a time, by winning at the one below.

[h2]In Arabic, properly[/h2]
The whole game plays in Arabic: the interface mirrors right to left, the text is shaped and laid out
for Arabic rather than reversed, and every card, enemy and tooltip is translated. Switchable at any
time from the main menu.

[h2]Plays how you like[/h2]
Mouse, keyboard or controller, with on-screen hints for whichever one you last touched.

## The content survey, answered honestly

Steamworks asks, and these are the answers for this build:

- **AI-generated content: yes, pre-generated.** Every card illustration, enemy portrait, background
  and icon in the game was generated locally with a Stable Diffusion XL checkpoint from prompts
  written for each piece, then hand-picked and filed by hand. No AI-generated content is created
  while the game runs, and no player input is sent anywhere. The disclosure Steam shows should say
  exactly that.
- **No violence beyond stylised fantasy combat**: numbers and coloured flashes, no blood, no gore.
- **No sexual content, no nudity, no drugs, no gambling, no user-generated content.**
- **No online features**, no telemetry, no accounts, nothing collected. The game never opens a
  network connection.
